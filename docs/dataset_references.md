# LightMap Dataset References & Citations

This document records the official sources, academic citations, provenance, and license terms for the benchmark datasets integrated into LightMap.

---

## 1. LOL (Low-Light) Dataset v1 (BMVC 2018)

- **Official Project Page**: [https://daooshee.github.io/BMVC2018website/](https://daooshee.github.io/BMVC2018website/)
- **Authors**: Chen Wei, Wenjing Wang, Wenhan Yang, Jiaying Liu (Peking University)
- **Paper**: *Deep Retinex Decomposition for Low-Light Enhancement*, British Machine Vision Conference (BMVC), 2018.
- **Description**: The pioneering real-world benchmark for Retinex-based low-light image enhancement. Consists of 500 paired images (485 training, 15 testing) captured by changing exposure time and ISO in stationary indoor scenes.
- **BibTeX Citation**:
  ```bibtex
  @inproceedings{wei2018deep,
    title={Deep Retinex Decomposition for Low-Light Enhancement},
    author={Wei, Chen and Wang, Wenjing and Yang, Wenhan and Liu, Jiaying},
    booktitle={British Machine Vision Conference (BMVC)},
    year={2018}
  }
  ```
- **License / Terms**: Non-commercial academic research use.

## 2. LOL-v2 Dataset (Real & Synthetic) (CVPR / IJCV 2021)

- **Official Kaggle Page**: [https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tanhyml/lol-v2-dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tanhyml/lol-v2-dataset)
- **Project Reference**: [https://github.com/flyywh/CVPR-2020-Semi-Low-Light](https://github.com/flyywh/CVPR-2020-Semi-Low-Light)
- **Authors**: Wenhan Yang, Wenjing Wang, Haofeng Huang, Shiqi Wang, Jiaying Liu
- **Paper**: *Benchmarking Low-Light Image Enhancement and Beyond*, International Journal of Computer Vision (IJCV), 2021 / IEEE CVPR.
- **Description**: An extended, diverse benchmark addressing the limitations of LOL-v1:
  - **LOL-v2-Real**: 689 training pairs, 100 testing pairs captured in real scenes with complex optical and sensor noise.
  - **LOL-v2-Synthetic**: 900 training pairs, 100 testing pairs generated by fitting real camera low-illumination noise distributions.
- **BibTeX Citation**:
  ```bibtex
  @article{yang2021benchmarking,
    title={Benchmarking Low-Light Image Enhancement and Beyond},
    author={Yang, Wenhan and Wang, Wenjing and Huang, Haofeng and Wang, Shiqi and Liu, Jiaying},
    journal={International Journal of Computer Vision (IJCV)},
    volume={129},
    number={4},
    pages={1153--1184},
    year={2021},
    publisher={Springer}
  }
  ```
- **License / Terms**: Academic research and benchmarking use.

## 3. MIT-Adobe FiveK Dataset (CVPR 2011)

- **Official Project Page**: [https://data.csail.mit.edu/graphics/fivek/](https://data.csail.mit.edu/graphics/fivek/)
- **Authors**: Vladimir Bychkovsky, Sylvain Paris, Eric Chan, Frédo Durand (MIT CSAIL & Adobe Systems)
- **Paper**: *Learning Photographic Global Tonal Adjustment with a Database of Input/Output Image Pairs*, IEEE Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR), 2011.
- **Description**: 5,000 raw photographs taken with SLR cameras, each retouched by 5 trained photography experts (Experts A, B, C, D, E). Used in LightMap as a supplementary evaluation reference for expert tonal adjustment, dynamic range preservation, and chromatic fidelity. Expert C is adopted as the primary ground-truth target.
- **BibTeX Citation**:
  ```bibtex
  @inproceedings{fivek,
    author = {Vladimir Bychkovsky and Sylvain Paris and Eric Chan and Fr{\'e}do Durand},
    title = {Learning Photographic Global Tonal Adjustment with a Database of Input / Output Image Pairs},
    booktitle = {IEEE Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR)},
    year = {2011}
  }
  ```
- **License / Terms**: Adobe-MIT FiveK License for research and education. Complete archive excluded from git tracking.
